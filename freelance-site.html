About

I think good design is mostly good decisions, made early.

I've spent the last six years working independently with founders, small studios, and a few in-house teams who needed an outside eye. Before that, I worked as a designer at a branding agency in Seattle, where I learned how much of the job is really about listening.

Every project starts the same way: understanding what the business actually needs to say, then building a system flexible enough to say it consistently — on a business card, a website, or a shipping box.
